Our 30-Day Hardware Guarantee

We build machines designed to be owned, not rented. If your SlateBook does not fit your engineering, development, or creative workflow, you may return the device within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full 100% refund. Because our machines feature accessible LPCAMM2 memory and standard M.2 storage slots, opening your chassis to inspect or upgrade hardware does not void your return rights or warranty.


Return Authorization Procedure

Please complete the following four steps before handing your parcel to Canada Post:

  1. Data Deprovisioning: Create an external backup of your personal files and execute a secure NVMe cryptographic erase via BIOS settings.
  2. Factory Hardware Restoration: If you installed aftermarket M.2 NVMe drives or secondary LPCAMM2 modules, replace the original stock hardware.
  3. Original Packaging: Repack the laptop into the recycled unbleached cardboard box alongside the braided magnetic Slate Connect cable and GaN power brick.
  4. Affix Shipping Label: Submit the authorization form below to generate and print your prepaid, insured priority shipping label.
