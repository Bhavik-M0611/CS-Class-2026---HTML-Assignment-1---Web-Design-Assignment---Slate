Proprietary Hardware Innovations

Purpose-built engineering designed for thermal longevity, modularity, and structural rigidity.


Slate Connect

Braided magnetic cable with a proprietary connector that allows for 140W charging, while keeping the Thunderbolt USB-C ports free for high-speed data transfer and external displays.

Liquid Metal Thermals

Liquid metal thermal interface material (TIM) for maximum heat transfer from the SoC to the vapour chamber and fans, providing zero thermal throttling and maximum sustained performance.

CNC Unibody Build

Precision CNC-milled aluminum chassis with a unibody design for maximum rigidity and durability, while keeping the weight low and the thermal performance high.
