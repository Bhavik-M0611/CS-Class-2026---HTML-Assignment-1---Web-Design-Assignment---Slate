About Slate Computing

Engineered for Longevity. Built Against Planned Obsolescence.


Our Philosophy

Slate Computing was founded with a single purpose: to create ultra-premium hardware that is built to last and is owned by the user. Over the past decade, major OEM brands have glued chassis shut, soldered RAM modules, and charged extremely high upgrade fees for basic storage upgrades. This makes the user unable to upgrade their own machines, and forces them to buy a new machine whenever their needs change. This is a form of planned obsolescence, and it is a practice that we at Slate Computing strongly oppose.

We take the opposite path. Every Slate laptop features a precision CNC-machined aluminum chassis, zero background telemetry daemons or bloatware, and fully accessible and hot-swappable internal components. Through our adoption of standard PCIe NVMe 2280 SSD slots and cutting-edge user-replaceable LPCAMM2 modules as well as being able to swap out components and even the entire motherboard, your machine remains upgradeable for years to come. If you search up our name on Google, you will find that we are also known as Slate Auto, which is a different division of our company, and we follow the same philosophy for our automotive-grade hardware.

If you would like to learn more about the memory that we use for our laptops, visit the JEDEC Solid State Technology Association.
